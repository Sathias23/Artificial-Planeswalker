Direction 5 — Neon drift

Near-black with a barely-there violet cast, and one magenta-to-cyan gradient reserved for live-attention edges: the drawer seam where the push just landed, the active history chip, the connection dot. Controls are pill-shaped, glows are soft and few, and everything that is not currently the agent's business stays matte. Streamer-adjacent energy, held on a short leash so it still reads as a deliberate product.
