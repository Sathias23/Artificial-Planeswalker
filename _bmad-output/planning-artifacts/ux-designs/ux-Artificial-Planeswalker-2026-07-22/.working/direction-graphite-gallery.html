Direction 2 — Graphite gallery

Achromatic graphite chrome with no accent color anywhere — the five mana colors and the card art are the only color on screen, hung on a dark wall with generous air and hairline dividers. Labels are small, tracked-out caps in the museum register; selection is carried by underlines and borders in the text color itself. The quietest direction: the app disappears and the deck is the exhibition.
