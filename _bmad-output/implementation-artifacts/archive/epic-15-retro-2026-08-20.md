### Diff-scope review (bmad-review: adversarial, edge-case-hunter, verification-gap)

All lens findings below were re-verified against primary sources before routing; unverified ones were dropped.

**R1 — CONFIRMED: the freeze self-test is red on a CRLF checkout.** (Also §Behavior verification.) `test_viewer_freeze.py:1168-1174` plants CRLF with `replace(b"\n", b"\r\n")`; on an `autocrlf=true` checkout `template.html` is already CRLF (235/235 lines), the plant yields `\r\r\n`, and the pin fires. The suite fails today on Brad's machine — on exactly the platform the test's docstring says it protects. **Disposition: fix now.** Make the plant idempotent (`re.sub(rb"\r?\n", b"\r\n", …)`) and/or give `template.html` a `.gitattributes` `eol` rule so one spelling exists everywhere.
