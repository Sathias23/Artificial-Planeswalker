**R3 — CONFIRMED (re-verified in source): revisiting an older push from the history popover regresses the kind's "latest" slot.** `reopenPush` delegates to `openAgentView`, which unconditionally writes `retained[kind] = content` (agentView.ts:452, :520-524).
